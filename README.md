# Vibe Tenders

> *"True power comes not from taking, but from giving; not from possessing, but from appreciating; not from conquering, but from cultivating spaces where everyone can shine."*

We are Vibe Tenders — conscious individuals who understand that the highest art is cultivating spaces where everyone can shine, love can multiply, and positive energy becomes a living force that elevates all.

## What is this place?

This repository is the living home of the Vibe Tenders Manifesto. Every version of this philosophy is permanently recorded here — you can scroll back through every edit and see how our thinking has evolved.

The manifesto is intentionally a living document. It grows through the input of our community, but its core principles are carefully protected. Proposals go through a stewardship process before anything changes.

## Navigating GitHub (if this is new to you)

You're looking at a project folder that tracks every change ever made to these documents.

- **To read a document:** click its name above (e.g. `MANIFESTO.md`)
- **To go back here:** click `vibe-tenders` in the breadcrumb at the top
- **To see the full history of a document:** open it, then click the clock/history icon near the top right
- **To see when something changed:** click "Commits" on this page

Nothing here is permanent in the sense of locked — everything can evolve. But every version is preserved.
