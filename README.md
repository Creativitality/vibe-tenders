## Membership: The Sacred Selection Process

### The Couple's Path (Primary Entry)
- **Couples Only**: New members must arrive as established partnerships
- **Unanimous Resonance**: Both partners must resonate with our existing energy
- **The Ladies' Council**: Female members hold special veto power over new female additions
- **The Brotherhood Vote**: Male members evaluate new male additions for Babe Minder potential
- **Trial Integration**: Potential members participate in 3+ group activities before formal acceptance

### The Single Exception (Rare Consideration)
- **Cross-Gender Sponsorship**: Single individuals require sponsorship and vote from opposite gender group members
- **Enhanced Vetting**: Extended trial period with higher standards
- **Energy Contribution**: Must demonstrate clear positive contribution to group dynamics
- **Partnership Trajectory**: Preference given to those actively seeking meaningful partnership

### The Guardian Principles
**Hire Slowly**: We take time to truly know potential members. No rushed decisions.

**Fire Fast**: If someone disrupts our energy or doesn't fit our values, we address it immediately and decisively.

**No Strays**: We don't bring random people to group events. Every guest must be carefully considered and often pre-approved.

**Quality Over Quantity**: We prioritize deepening existing bonds over expansion. Growth only happens when we encounter truly special couples who elevate our entire formation.

**The Veto Power**: Any core member can pause the consideration of new members if they sense misalignment.
